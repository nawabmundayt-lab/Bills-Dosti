# 📣 Phase 8 — Launch & Marketing Plan

> Status: **plan ready** · launch day is the finish line of the 23-week roadmap.

## 1. Positioning (one-liner to use everywhere)

> **"The hisaab app for doston in Pakistan & India — settle with one tap via JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast or UPI. Money never touches us."**

## 2. Pre-launch (Week 22)

| Channel | Action | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Founder story reels: "How we used to fight over chai money" → product reveal | 3×/week |
| WhatsApp | Share invite-link teases in friend groups + university/office circles | organic |
| Facebook Groups | Lahore/Karachi/Islamabad renters & flatmate groups: "flatmate hisaab made easy" | 2×/week |
| Reddit | r/Pakistan, r/karachi, r/bangalore: honest "I built this" posts w/ PWA link | launch week |
| TikTok/Reels | "POV: your flatmate owes you 3 months of chai" skits | 3×/week |
| Influencers | 3–5 micro-influencers (10–50k, PK/IN students) — budget Rs 20–50k total | weeks 22–23 |

**Install funnel:** every post links to `https://billsplitdost.pk` → PWA install
prompt (no Play Store gate for early access!) — a key advantage of the PWA strategy.

## 3. Launch day (Week 23)

- [ ] 09:00 — WhatsApp broadcast to 50+ personal contacts w/ invite links

- [ ] 12:00 — Influencer posts go live
- [ ] 15:00 — Live "Ask me anything" on Instagram — settle a live demo with a friend
- [ ] 20:00 — Day-1 numbers review: installs, activation, first expense rate
- [ ] Continuous — support WhatsApp (+92 300 1234567) monitored; P0 fixes same-day
